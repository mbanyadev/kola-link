Kola Link

A self-sovereign identity that binds a Bitcoin Cash wallet to a Nostr key — so an app can recognise you, and someone can pay you, without a central account.

The problem

Digital identity still sits behind platforms.

Accounts, logins and “send it to my address” all depend on someone else’s database, or on pasting long strings into chat. Bitcoin Cash and Nostr already give people keys. They are not yet tied together in a way other apps can verify.

The idea

One statement, two proofs of control.

The holder signs a canonical kola-link:v1 statement with a Nostr key and with a Bitcoin Cash key. The keys are independent. Putting an address in a Nostr profile is not enough.

How it works

Discoverable, but not hosted by us.

  • A kind-30078 event is published to public relays.
  • Anyone can look it up by npub or by cashaddr.
  • Verification is a pure function: Nostr signature, statement commitment, recovered Bitcoin Cash key.
  • A relying app such as Sawa Market logs the holder in with a short-lived challenge.
Demo

From keys to a verified login in about a minute.

The live demo is client-side and defaults to chipnet. You can generate demo keys in the browser, or bring a NIP-07 signer and a WizardConnect wallet (Paytaca, Selene, Cashonize).

Stretch

Pay a person, not a raw address.

  • Resolve an npub to a verified cashaddr.
  • Kumba Verify issues salted attestations; the holder discloses one attribute at a time.
  • An optional on-chain XO identity-anchor timestamps the commitment and can be rotated or revoked.
Build with XO

A novel identity-anchor template.

Actions: bind, rotate, revoke. The redeem script commits to sha256(statement) and still needs the holder’s signature. Designed and tested for chipnet. Invitation reproduction is in the repository.
